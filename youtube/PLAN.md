# YouTube Channel Plan 🎥

## Channel ka idea
**Pakistani logon ko AI (Claude) ke zariye website banana aur freelancing se kamana sikhana, Urdu mein.**

Tumhari sab se badi taqat: tum **khud ye safar kar rahe ho.** Tum "expert" ban ke nahi, **"mere saath seekho"** ke andaz mein dikhate ho. Log aisi asli kahani pe zyada bharosa karte hain.

## Naam
**`DesignSpark`** (handle: `@designsparkpk` ya jo mile)
- Logo, rang aur brand pehle se tayyar hain
- Fiverr profile aur channel ek hi brand ke hon, to channel dekhne wale **clients** bhi ban sakte hain

## Chehra dikhana zaroori nahi ✅
**Faceless channel:** screen recording + tumhari Urdu awaaz. Pakistan mein bohot se tech channels aise hi chalte hain.
- **Saman:** phone ya laptop, aur ek sasta mic ya earphones ka mic. Shuru mein phone ka mic bhi theek hai (khamosh kamre mein record karo).
- **Screen recording:** laptop pe OBS (free), phone pe built-in screen recorder.
- **Editing:** CapCut (free).

## Schedule
- **Har hafte 1 lambi video** (8-15 minute) + **3 Shorts**
- **Ek din fix karo**, jaise har Sunday. Consistency views se zyada zaroori hai.

## Paisa kab aayega? (sach)
- **YouTube ads ka paisa** ke liye YouTube Partner Program ki shartein poori karni hoti hain (aam taur pe **1,000 subscribers** + **4,000 ghante** watch time, ya Shorts views ka bada target). YouTube ye rules badalta rehta hai, is liye apply karte waqt check karna. Is mein **6-12 mahine** lag sakte hain.
- **Jaldi faida in raaston se hoga:**
  1. **Clients:** channel dekh ke log Fiverr pe kaam dein
  2. **Course ya class:** 1-2 hazaar subscribers ke baad apna paid course (option #6 wala)
  3. **Affiliate links:** jo tools tum use karte ho, unke links description mein

⚠️ **Ye "jaldi paisa" wala raasta nahi hai.** Isay Fiverr ke **saath** chalao, us ki jagah nahi.
