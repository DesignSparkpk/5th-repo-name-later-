# Bahar ke Desi Restaurants: Outreach Plan 🌍

**Kyun:** UK, Dubai aur Canada mein hazaron Pakistani restaurants hain. Owners Urdu bolte hain, website ki ahmiyat samajhte hain, aur £150-400 dena unke liye normal hai.

**Sab se bara hook:** UK mein Just Eat, Uber Eats aur Deliveroo har order pe **25-35% commission** lete hain. Tumhari website pe customer **seedha WhatsApp pe order** karta hai, aur commission **zero**. Ye owner ki jeb ki baat hai, to wo sunta hai.

---

## Roz ka routine (1 ghanta)

1. **10 restaurants dhoondo** (neeche tareeqa)
2. **`tracker.csv`** mein unka naam, sheher aur Instagram ya email likho
3. Unhe **personal message** bhejo (neeche templates)
4. 3 din purane messages ka **follow-up** bhejo

Roz 10, yani hafte mein 50. In mein se 2-5 reply aayein to bhi kaafi hai. **Ek client = 3-4 Fiverr orders ke barabar.**

---

## Restaurants kaise dhoondein

Google Maps pe ye search karo:
- `Pakistani restaurant Bradford` · `Birmingham` · `Manchester` · `Glasgow` · `Leicester` · `Luton` · `Slough`
- `Desi restaurant Dubai` · `Karahi Toronto` · `Pakistani BBQ Mississauga`

**Achha target (✅):**
- Website **nahi hai**, sirf Facebook ya Just Eat ka link
- Website hai lekin **purani, toot-phooti ya mobile pe kharab**
- Reviews achhe hain (4+ stars), yani business chal raha hai aur paisa hai

**Chhod do (❌):**
- Bari chains (Mughli, Shimla Pinks waghaira): inki marketing team hoti hai
- Jinki website already zabardast hai

**Contact kahan milega:** Google Maps pe Instagram ya Facebook ka link, ya website pe email.

---

## Message kaise bhejein

- **Instagram DM ya Facebook page message** sab se behtar hai, kyunki owner khud dekhta hai.
- **Email** bhi theek hai.
- ❌ **WhatsApp pe cold message mat bhejo.** Ajeeb lagta hai aur number block ho sakta hai.
- ❌ **Ek hi message 50 logon ko copy-paste mat karo.** Har message mein restaurant ka naam aur ek cheez jo tumne unke baare mein dekhi, zaroor likho.
- ❌ Agar koi kahe "not interested", to **dobara message mat karo**.

## Jab koi "haan" kahe 🎉

1. **Mujhe batao.** Hum unke menu aur photos se ek **preview** banayenge (1-2 din).
2. **Price:** £150-250 (simple site) · £250-400 (full menu + ordering + hosting)
3. **Payment kaise lein: Fiverr ke zariye!** Client ko kaho: *"For your safety, we'll do the payment through Fiverr."* Phir unhe apne gig ka link ya **custom offer** bhejo.
   - ✅ Client ke paise **safe** rehte hain, is liye wo bharosa karta hai
   - ✅ Tumhe Fiverr pe **review** milta hai, jisse doosre orders bhi aate hain
   - ✅ Paisa Payoneer ke zariye Pakistan mein aata hai
   - Fiverr 20% leta hai, lekin reviews aur bharose ke liye ye shuru mein faida mand hai

⚠️ **Fiverr ka rule:** jo client **Fiverr pe mila**, use kabhi Fiverr se bahar mat le jao. Lekin jo client **tum khud bahar se laye ho**, use Fiverr pe order karwana bilkul allowed hai.

## Yaad rakho
- Pehle hafte koi reply na aaye to **normal** hai. 50 messages ke baad dekhna.
- Jo reply kare, chahe "no" kahe, us se **izzat se** baat karo.
- Har reply aur sawal **yahan Claude ke paas le aao**, saath mil ke jawab likhenge.
