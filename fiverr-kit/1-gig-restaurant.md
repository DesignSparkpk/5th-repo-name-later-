## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: Do you use WordPress?**
A: No. I build with clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so your site is very fast, secure, and cheap to host (often free).

**Q: Will my website work on mobile phones?**
A: Yes. Every website is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

**Q: Can customers order food through the website?**
A: Yes. Customers pick dishes and the order is sent to your WhatsApp with their name and address. No monthly app fees.

**Q: Do you provide hosting and a domain?**
A: Hosting setup is included in the Premium package (free hosting on Netlify or Cloudflare Pages). The domain name is bought separately by you, and I'll help you connect it.

**Q: What do you need from me?**
A: Your restaurant name, logo, menu with prices, photos, address, phone/WhatsApp number and opening hours.

**Q: Can I update the menu later?**
A: Yes. The menu is easy to edit, and I can also make updates for you later as a small order.
