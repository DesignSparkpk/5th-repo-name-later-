## FAQ
**Q:** `I don't have a CV. Can you still help?`
**A:** `Yes. Just send your education, work experience and skills, and I will create your CV from scratch.`

**Q:** `What is an ATS-friendly CV?`
**A:** `Many companies use software (ATS) to scan CVs before a human sees them. I use a clean layout and the right keywords so your CV gets through.`

**Q:** `Will I get an editable file?`
**A:** `Yes. You get an editable Word file and a ready-to-send PDF.`

**Q:** `Can you write a CV for jobs abroad?`
**A:** `Yes. Tell me the country and job, and I will follow the style employers there expect.`
