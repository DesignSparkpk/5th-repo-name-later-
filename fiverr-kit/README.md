## Din 1: Account banao (1-2 ghante)

1. **fiverr.com** pe jao → "Join" → apni email se account banao.
2. "Become a Seller" pe jao.
3. **Personal info:** asli naam, asli photo, aur `3-profile.md` se description copy karo.
4. **Professional info:** skills aur languages `3-profile.md` se dalo.
5. **Account security:** phone number verify karo.
6. **ID verification:** Fiverr CNIC maang sakta hai. Asli CNIC do, naam profile wala hi hona chahiye.

> ⚠️ **Ek insaan, ek account.** Doosra account kabhi mat banana, dono band ho jayenge. Kisi aur ke saath ek hi WiFi/phone pe Fiverr account chalana bhi masla kar sakta hai.

## Din 1-2: Pehla gig live karo

1. Profile → "Gigs" → "Create a New Gig".
2. `1-gig-restaurant.md` kholo aur har section copy-paste karte jao:
   **Overview → Pricing → Description & FAQ → Requirements → Gallery → Publish**
3. Gallery mein `gig-images/` ki 3 images is order mein lagao: `1-cover.jpg`, `2-showcase.jpg`, `3-mobile.jpg`.
4. **Publish!** 🎉

## Din 2-3: Portfolio daalo
Profile ke "Portfolio" section mein `portfolio/` ki 5 images dalo (titles `3-profile.md` mein hain). Har ek ke saath **"Concept"** zaroor likho.

## Paise kaise nikalenge (Pakistan)
Fiverr pe kamaai pehle **14 din "pending"** rehti hai (naye sellers ke liye), phir withdraw ho sakti hai. Pakistan mein aam tareeqe:
- **Payoneer** → wahan se Pakistani bank account mein
- Fiverr ka **direct bank transfer** (agar tumhare account mein option aaye)

Payoneer account abhi se bana lo, approval mein kuch din lagte hain.

## 💙 Sach baat, dil se

- **Naye account pe pehla order aane mein 2 se 6 hafte lag sakte hain.** Agar 2 hafte mein order na aaye to ye normal hai, tumhari galti nahi. Har seller ka shuru aisa hi hota hai.
- **Pehle 3-5 orders sab se mushkil hote hain.** Unke baad Fiverr tumhe khud buyers dikhane lagta hai.
- **Ek bura review bohot nuksaan karta hai.** Is liye `5-order-delivery.md` wali checklist kabhi skip mat karna, aur jo kaam tum check na kar sako wo order mat lena.
- Fiverr ke saath **local kaam bhi chalate raho** (CV, Excel, chhote kaam) taake is dauraan ghar ka kharcha chalta rahe.
- **Scams se bacho:** koi kahe "pehle fee do, phir order dunga", "WhatsApp pe aao", ya "Fiverr ke bahar pay karunga", to ye 100% fraud hai.

Tumhari 5 websites professional quality ki hain. Bas ab inhein sahi logon tak pohnchana hai. 💪
