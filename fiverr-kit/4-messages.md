# Ready Messages (copy-paste)

> `[ ]` wali jagah apni detail bharo. **Har message thoda personal bana lo** (buyer ka naam, unke business ka naam). Fiverr pe **jitna jaldi reply karo utna achha**: response time profile pe dikhta hai. Fiverr app phone pe install kar lo aur notifications on rakho.

---

### 1. Jab koi pehli baar message kare
```
Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. 😊

I'd love to help with your [restaurant/business] website. To give you the best suggestion, could you tell me:

1. What is your business name and what do you sell?
2. Do you already have a logo, menu and photos?
3. Do you have a website you like the style of?
4. When do you need it ready?

Once I know this, I'll recommend the right package for you.
```

### 2. Package suggest karna
```
Thanks for the details! Based on what you need, I recommend the [Standard] package ($[85]).

It includes:
• [Full online menu with prices]
• [WhatsApp ordering button]
• [Gallery and Google Maps]
• Mobile-friendly design
• [3] revisions

Delivery in [5] days. If you'd like, I can send you a custom offer right now.
```

### 3. Custom offer bhejte waqt
```
Here's your custom offer. It includes everything we discussed. Once you accept, please share your logo, menu and photos, and I'll get started right away. 🚀
```

### 4. Order shuru hone pe
```
Thank you for your order, [Name]! 🎉

I've received your files and I'm starting on your website now. I'll share a progress update within [2] days. If you think of anything else, just message me here.
```

### 5. Progress update (beech mein)
```
Hi [Name], quick update: the main design is ready and I'm now working on the [menu / gallery] section. Everything is on track for delivery by [day]. 👍
```

### 6. Delivery ke waqt
```
Hi [Name], your website is ready! 🎉

I've attached:
• The complete website files (ZIP)
• Screenshots on desktop and mobile
• A short guide on how to put it online

Please check everything and let me know if you'd like any changes. I'm happy to adjust anything until you're 100% satisfied.
```

### 7. Revision maangi ho
```
Sure, no problem! I'll make these changes:
• [change 1]
• [change 2]

I'll send the updated version within [24 hours].
```

### 8. Order complete hone pe (review ke liye)
```
Thank you so much for working with me, [Name]! It was a pleasure building your website.

If you're happy with the result, I'd really appreciate it if you could leave a review. It helps my small business a lot. 🙏

If you ever need updates or a new page, I'm always here to help.
```

### 9. Buyer kuch aisa maange jo tum nahi kar sakte
```
Thanks for explaining! To be honest, [this feature] is outside what I offer right now, and I don't want to promise something I can't deliver perfectly. I can do [what you CAN do] instead. Would that work for you?
```

---

## ⚠️ Fiverr ke rules (inhein todne se account band ho jata hai)
- ❌ **Fiverr se bahar contact mat maango ya do**: WhatsApp number, email, Skype, kuch bhi nahi. Ye sab se bara rule hai.
- ❌ **Fiverr se bahar payment mat lo.**
- ❌ **Review ke badle discount ya paisa mat do**, aur "5 star dena" mat kaho. Sirf "please leave a review" kaho.
- ✅ Saari baat-cheet Fiverr inbox mein karo.
